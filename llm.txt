- Used LLM tool: 
ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot

- Motivation / reason to use LLM:
Stuck in setting up a Kotlin & gradle project as it is a new language
Copilot is enabled in my IDEs in the hopes that it saves time by not having to write duplicate and menial code. Also helps with debugging

- How and why LLM helped: 
ChatGPT was especially helpful with debugging what was wrong with my Kotlin/gradle setup. 
Github Copilot in turn tries to be helpful with autofilling simple bits of code, but more often than not it seems to autofill completely unrelated or broken bits of code.

- What kind of mistakes LLM did:
Both LLMs frequently made mistakes. Even though ChatGPT was able to eventually debug my Kotlin/Gradle setup, it took extensive propmpting to reach that point.
Copilot in turn tries to autofill a line (or lines) of code that you are writing. Often times, however, it writes way too complicated or complitely unrelated code. I think the problem is its lack of context, as it can not fully understand the end goal and/or the vision of the programmer.
This often leads to wasting time juggling between tab/esq/backspace keyes as it proposes autofill -> you dont want it to autofill -> it autofills anyways -> you need to erase what it wrote... (having said that, there are times when it gets things right and actually slightly increases overall efficiency)

- What were things that LLM was not able to provide:
I think the most important parts were already said above.
